Typography
Use a modern geometric sans-serif — Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans. Headlines bold and large. Body text regular weight, comfortable line height. Gradient text effects on key headlines using the brand gradient.

Design Style
Clean, minimal, and premium. Not corporate — modern tech with warmth. Generous white space. Subtle gradient backgrounds on hero and CTA sections. Smooth card shadows. Rounded corners — 12px to 16px radius on cards. Gradient accents and glows used sparingly for visual hierarchy. The site should feel like a sophisticated, confident product — not a startup template.

Navigation
Sticky header. Logo left. Nav links center — Features, Integrations, AI First, Pricing, About, Developer, Contact. Login and Sign Up buttons right. Sign Up button uses brand gradient as background.

Hero Section
Full-width hero with a subtle gradient background from white to a very light cyan-purple tint. Large bold headline with gradient text on the key phrase. Subheadline in grey. Two CTA buttons — primary with gradient fill, secondary with outline. Right side: animated mockup of the omnichannel inbox showing all five channel icons flowing into one unified dashboard.

Feature Sections
Alternating layout — text left image right, then image left text right. Feature cards use white background with subtle shadow and a thin gradient border on hover. Icons use the brand gradient as fill.

AI Section
Dark section — deep navy or dark purple background. Three feature cards with gradient borders and subtle glow effects. Feels powerful and intelligent.

Pricing Section
Three plan cards on white background. Middle card — Growth Plan — uses gradient border and subtle background tint to indicate recommended. Price displayed large and bold. Feature list with checkmarks in brand teal.

Footer
Dark background — deep navy. Logo white. Four column layout — Quick Links, Explore, Legal, Contact. Social icons. Copyright line. WhatsApp floating button bottom right — brand gradient background.

Responsive
Fully mobile responsive. On mobile, navigation collapses to hamburger. Hero stacks vertically. Feature cards stack to single column. Pricing cards stack vertically with recommended plan first.

Tone
Confident. Professional. AI-forward. Not clinical — human and approachable. The design should communicate that Trevio is a premium product trusted by serious businesses.
